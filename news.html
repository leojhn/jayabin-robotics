Marykutty Junior at BioConnect 4.0

We were delighted to participate in BioConnect 4.0 at Hyatt Regency, Thiruvananthapuram, with sponsorship support from Kerala Medical Technology Consortium (KMTC). The event provided an excellent opportunity to introduce Marykutty Junior, the first product in our growing technology ecosystem, designed to advance hospital automation through robotics and artificial intelligence. During the event, we showcased our solution to attendees, industry professionals, and innovators, sharing our vision for smarter, more efficient, and technology-driven healthcare environments. Marykutty Junior aims to support hospital operations through intelligent interaction and automation, contributing to a smoother experience for patients and healthcare professionals. The event offered a valuable platform to exchange ideas, explore emerging technologies, and connect with potential collaborators. This milestone marks an important step in our journey toward building an integrated ecosystem of intelligent solutions. We believe that collaboration drives innovation, and we look forward to partnering with healthcare institutions, industry experts, and technology innovators to shape the future of hospital automation. We are grateful to KMTC for its support in enabling our participation in this event.
