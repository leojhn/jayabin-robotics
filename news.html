Marykutty Junior at BioConnect 4.0

We were delighted to participate in BioConnect 4.0 at Hyatt Regency, Thiruvananthapuram, where we introduced Marykutty Junior, the first product in our ecosystem, designed to advance hospital automation through robotics and artificial intelligence. During the event, we showcased our solution to attendees, industry professionals, and innovators, sharing our vision for smarter, more efficient, and technology-driven healthcare environments. Marykutty Junior aims to support hospital operations through intelligent interaction and automation, contributing to a more seamless experience for both patients and healthcare professionals. The event provided a valuable platform to exchange ideas, explore emerging technologies, and connect with potential collaborators. This milestone marks an important step in our journey toward building an integrated ecosystem of intelligent solutions. We believe that collaboration drives innovation, and we look forward to partnering with healthcare institutions, industry experts, and technology innovators to shape the future of hospital automation.
